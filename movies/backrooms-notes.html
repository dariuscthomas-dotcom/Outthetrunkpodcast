Show Notes & Highlights

Best Question:

Why did this movie become popular when the hosts found almost no story, character development, or payoff?

Jordan Insight:

The film feels like a maze without a destination. Every time a theme appears, the movie abandons it before developing a meaningful journey.

Darius Insight:

The Backrooms works better as a short-form internet concept than a feature-length film. Stretching a YouTube idea into two hours exposed the lack of story. Best Debate: Is the movie a deep metaphor about the human mind, or simply an incoherent story that audiences are overanalyzing?

Major Themes:

Isolation; loneliness; mental health; liminal spaces; identity; meaning-making; storytelling versus concept. Archive Note: This became the lowest-rated movie in Out The Trunk history, earning a combined 0.5-star average and being labeled the worst film the show has reviewed. Notable Discussion Topics: Why viral internet concepts do not always translate into movies; A24 expectations; lack of dialogue; confusing ending; therapist scenes; YouTube-to-Hollywood adaptations; storytelling fundamentals; worst movie reviewed debate.
