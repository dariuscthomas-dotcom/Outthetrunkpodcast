Show Notes & Highlights

Best Question:

Should Michael have pursued a serious relationship with Mae while actively preparing to take a job in another country?

Jordan Insight:

The movie contains the ingredients for a meaningful story about Black motherhood, ambition, generational wounds, and family legacy, but never fully commits to developing any of those ideas.

Darius Insight:

The film feels like two separate movies stitched together. The flashbacks and present-day romance rarely connect in a meaningful way, leaving both storylines underdeveloped. Best Debate Was this a genuine romance, or was it simply a less compelling attempt to recreate the magic of Love Jones for a newer generation?
