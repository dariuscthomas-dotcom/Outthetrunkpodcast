Show Notes & Highlights

Topics Covered

  • Dating apps and why the incentives are broken
  • Chain restaurants and international fast-food exceptions
  • Which technology should be deleted forever
  • Art, legacy, critics, and the fear of releasing work
  • Good-bad movies and critic vs fan expectations
  • Historic sporting events worth time traveling for

Best Questions

  • If you could delete one technological advancement forever, what would it be?
  • What is the point of making art if nobody ever sees it?
  • If you could attend one sporting event in history, what would it be?

Best Observations

  • Smartphones are framed as a net negative because the internet moved from the room into the pocket.
  • Do not let your art die with you. Your work can outlive your actual life.
  • Critics and fans serve different functions, and a bad critical score does not always erase fan enjoyment.

Best Debate

Technology deletion: Jordan chooses consumer pickup trucks because of road behavior and safety; Darius chooses smartphones because of attention, information overload, and the social cost of pocket internet.
