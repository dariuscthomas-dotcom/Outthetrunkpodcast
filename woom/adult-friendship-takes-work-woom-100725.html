Adult Friendship Takes Work (WOOM 10/07/25)

Show Notes & Highlights

Topics Covered

  • Coconut tree trick question
  • Making friends as an adult
  • Third spaces and adult community
  • Going out alone
  • Church, barbershops, book clubs, gyms, and social muscles

Best Questions

  • How do adults make friends?
  • What institutions still create community?
  • Is going out alone a skill people need to relearn?

Best Observations

  • Adult friendship takes intentionality because school and college no longer create automatic proximity.
  • Third spaces matter because they give people repeated low-pressure contact.
  • If you want community, you have to participate in it.

Best Debate

Adult friendship: the conversation centers on whether people lack opportunity, courage, institutions, or repeated practice.

Major Themes

  • Friendship
  • Community

  • Third spaces
  • Social skills
