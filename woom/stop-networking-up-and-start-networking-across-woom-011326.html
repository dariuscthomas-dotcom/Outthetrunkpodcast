Show Notes & Highlights

Topics Covered

  • Grammar correction and social etiquette

  • Talent clusters and creative ecosystems

Best Questions

  • Friendship can be tested more by success than failure.

Best Debate

Success and friendship: the core tension is whether ambition strengthens friendships through shared growth or strains them through envy, distance, and comparison.
