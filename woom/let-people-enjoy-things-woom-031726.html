Show Notes & Highlights

Topics Covered

  • Let people enjoy things
  • Anime kids and childhood bullying discourse
  • Michael B. Jordan stories and kindness
  • Bullying, roasting, and character building
  • Nerd culture winning mainstream culture

Best Questions

  • When should you just let people enjoy things?
  • At what point do childhood grievances need to be retired?
  • Is helpful roasting different from bullying?

Best Observations

  • Sometimes there is no benefit in telling someone the thing they love is trash.
  • Nerd culture won so thoroughly that some old grievances need updating.
  • When actual smart people speak, they often know how to make others comfortable with the intelligence.

Best Debate

Bullying/roasting: the conversation plays with whether social pressure builds character or simply becomes people carrying old wounds into adulthood.
