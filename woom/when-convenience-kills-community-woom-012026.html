When Convenience Kills Community (WOOM 01/20/26)

Show Notes & Highlights

Topics Covered

  • Snow day activities
  • Reliability as a success trait
  • Antisocial things that feel pro-social
  • Self-checkout and club sections
  • Efficiency vs community

Best Questions

  • Are successful people successful because they are reliable?
  • What things are antisocial but feel pro-social?
  • Does every convenience make life better?

Best Observations

  • Reliable people tend to be successful people.
  • Everything does not have to be optimized for efficiency.
  • Convenience can quietly remove the small social interactions that make community feel real.

Best Debate

Efficiency vs community: some innovations save time, but they can also remove contact, friction, and the social glue of shared spaces.
