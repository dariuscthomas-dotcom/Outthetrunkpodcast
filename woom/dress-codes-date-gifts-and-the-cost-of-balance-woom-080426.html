Best Observations

  • Comfort does not have to be the highest priority in every public or professional setting.
  • Less television and scrolling may create time, but demanding leisure still carries a cognitive cost.
  • Weekends can function as both a reward and a soft reset into the next week.
  • Older classics can lose some of their original impact when once-revolutionary effects become familiar.

Best Debate

Tuskegee dress code: both supported basic classroom standards such as avoiding sleepwear, bonnets, and durags, while agreeing that extending the same rules to the cafeteria goes too far.
