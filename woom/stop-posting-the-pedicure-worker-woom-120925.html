Stop Posting the Pedicure Worker (WOOM 12/09/25)

Show Notes & Highlights

Topics Covered

  • Pedicure posts and class discomfort
  • Service workers being photographed
  • Social media performance of luxury/service
  • Family and holiday culture

Best Questions

  • Is it off-putting to post service workers doing personal care labor?
  • When does documenting a treat become classless?
  • How should people share luxury or self-care without objectifying workers?

Best Observations

  • Some “self-care” posts accidentally frame service workers as props.
  • The after-photo is usually less uncomfortable than showing the worker doing the labor.
  • A small social media habit can reveal class, privacy, and dignity issues.

Best Debate

Pedicure posting: Darius finds the labor photo off-putting; Jordan agrees that the after-photo is cleaner and less exploitative.

Major Themes

  • Class
  • Social media
  • Service work
  • Etiquette
  • Self-presentation
