Smartphones Are the New Cigarettes (WOOM 08/25/26)

Show Notes & Highlights

Topics Covered

  • Dream homes, luxury amenities, and assembling the perfect house
  • What makes a compelling protagonist in television and film
  • Competence versus likability in storytelling
  • Characters they disliked despite loving the show
  • Mike Ross, Michael Scott, Walter White, and other complicated protagonists
  • Chris Hayes’ Sirens’ Call analogy and the modern attention economy
  • Smartphones as the cigarettes of the modern era
  • Systems for reducing distractions and improving focus
  • Dumb phones, print newspapers, phone-free walks, and reclaiming boredom
  • Whether past cultural moral panics were right all along
  • What they are looking forward to this fall
  • Small victories, motivation, and maintaining morale

Best Questions

  • What makes a good protagonist?
  • Can an unlikable protagonist still carry a great story?
  • How do you “tie yourself to the mast” when distractions are everywhere?
  • Are smartphones today’s equivalent of cigarettes?
  • What are you looking forward to over the next few months?
  • How important are small victories when life is not going according to plan?

Best Observations

  • Competence can matter more than likability when creating a compelling protagonist.
  • Many stories weaken when the main character avoids meaningful decisions.
  • Smartphones constantly seek attention while turning distraction into revenue.
  • Learning to be bored again may be one of the most valuable skills of the digital age.
  • Some past cultural moral panics contained more truth than people wanted to admit.
  • Progress is easier to sustain when it includes small rewards and milestones.

Best Debate

Are smartphones the cigarettes of the modern era? Darius argued that future generations may view constant phone use the way people now view smoking in restaurants and airplanes. Jordan agreed that endless stimulation and shortened attention spans are producing consequences society has not fully confronted.
